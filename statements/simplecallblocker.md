# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 21, 2026

## Introduction

Simple Call Blocker is a simple, open-source Android application for blocking unwanted incoming calls from numbers that are not in the user's contacts list.

## Call Screening

Simple Call Blocker uses Android's CallScreeningService to screen incoming calls.

Android determines which incoming calls are presented to the app's call-screening service. Calls from numbers that are not in the user's contacts list may be presented to the service, allowing the app to reject them.

The app does not access or read the user's contacts.

## Data Collection

Simple Call Blocker does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal or sensitive user data.

Information about incoming calls received by the call-screening service is used only to perform the app's call-blocking functionality and is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

## Data Sharing

Simple Call Blocker does not share user information with the developer or any third party.

The app does not contain online services, advertising, analytics, or other functionality intended to collect user information.

## Internet Usage

Simple Call Blocker does not require an internet connection to perform its functionality.

The app does not transmit user information over the internet.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Simple Call Blocker does not maintain a separate collection of personal or sensitive user data. Call information received for screening is used only for the screening operation and is not retained by the app.

Because the app does not collect or maintain personal user data, there is no app-maintained personal data to delete.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data-handling practices change.

Any updated version will be published at the same location as this policy.
